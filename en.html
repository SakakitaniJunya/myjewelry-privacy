Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

"My Jewelry" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. No account registration is required.

2. Data Storage

All jewelry information, photos, and records you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to external servers.

3. Network Communication

The App sends read-only requests to product page URLs that you provide, solely to retrieve price, image, and page title information. These requests contain no personal information. No other external communication occurs.

4. Third Parties and Analytics

The App uses no analytics tools or advertising SDKs and shares no information with third parties.

5. Photos and Other Permissions

Photo library access is used only when you select jewelry images.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact listed on the App Store seller page.
